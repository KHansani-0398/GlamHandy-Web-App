Privacy Policy

How GlamHandy protects, handles, and respects your personal data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to GlamHandy Arts & Crafts ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our customers and visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you visit or make a purchase from GlamHandy.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or place an order for craft materials, we may collect the following types of information:

    Personal Details: Your full name, email address, phone number, and shipping address. Account Credentials: Username, password hash, and order preferences. Order History: Details of craft supplies purchased, payment confirmations, and cart activity. Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device details, and browsing cookies to enhance page responsiveness.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information for the following specific purposes:

    To process, dispatch, and fulfill your craft order shipments smoothly. To communicate order tracking updates, customer support support, and invoices. To personalize your shopping experience across our crochet, yarn, and DIY craft collections. To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions or legal violations.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Your sensitive account data is encrypted using standard protocol protections.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

GlamHandy uses local browser storage and cookie technologies to keep track of items added to your shopping cart and remember login sessions. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some website features may not function properly.
